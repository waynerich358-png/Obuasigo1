Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Food and product orders

Cancellation and refund eligibility should depend on the order status, partner acceptance, preparation status and the reason for the request. The final operational rules must be configured by ObuasiGo and participating vendors before launch.

Hotel bookings

Hotel cancellation and refund terms may differ by hotel and room rate. The applicable conditions should be shown to the customer before payment.

Failed payments

If a payment is charged but the platform does not confirm the transaction, the payment provider transaction reference should be investigated before issuing or confirming a refund.

How to request help

Use the support channel provided in the live app and include the order or booking reference.
