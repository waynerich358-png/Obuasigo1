Cookies & Tracking

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Necessary storage

The current web application uses browser storage for things such as authentication sessions, shopping cart state and privacy preferences. These functions are required for the app to operate.

Optional analytics

Optional analytics is disabled until the visitor chooses “Accept analytics” in the privacy banner. If analytics is configured, the application can load the configured analytics service after consent.

Third-party services

Payment, OTP, maps, hosting, storage and notification providers may process information as part of the requested service. Their own policies also apply.

Your choice

Visitors can decline optional analytics. The live production configuration should document the exact technologies, retention periods and choices available.
