Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page describes how ObuasiGo intends to handle information used to provide accounts, orders, deliveries, hotel bookings, partner verification and support.

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, phone number and email address.
  • Delivery and booking information needed to fulfil a service.
  • Partner verification information and documents submitted by riders, vendors and hotels.
  • Location information when a user chooses to provide it for delivery or tracking.
  • Payment transaction references and status. Card credentials should be handled by the selected payment provider rather than stored by ObuasiGo.
  • Optional analytics information only after the user's analytics choice is accepted.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, fulfil orders and bookings, process payments, provide delivery tracking, verify partners, prevent abuse, provide support, maintain security and improve the service.

Sharing

Information may be shared with the vendor, hotel, rider, payment provider, OTP provider, hosting/storage provider or other service provider only when necessary to provide the requested service or operate the platform. ObuasiGo should not sell personal information to third parties.

Security

Production systems should use HTTPS, server-side authorization, secure secret storage, access controls, audit logs and private document storage. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and requests

Retention periods and account deletion procedures should be finalized before launch and documented for the actual operating company.

Contact

Replace this section before launch: insert the real registered business name, physical/business address, support email and privacy contact.

This policy is a product template, not legal advice. Have the final policy reviewed for the jurisdictions in which ObuasiGo operates before collecting real identity documents or launching publicly.
